title: Cloud & Delivery
---

I care about how software is built, deployed and operated, not just how it works on a developer's machine. I have experience with **AWS serverless functions**, CDN-hosted sites and automated delivery pipelines, and I value cloud-native approaches that keep services scalable and maintainable.

I've worked with GitHub Actions, Azure Pipelines, Atlassian Pipelines and Travis to automate build, test and deployment workflows. I also use testing and static analysis as part of delivery, including Jest, Playwright, PHPUnit, PHPStan and SonarQube.
